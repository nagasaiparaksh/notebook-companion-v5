# Review: intro (writing → typing → touch → voice)

## Creative calls
- The storyline was given in full by the brief, so no alternative storylines were drafted.
- One continuous scene (`compositions/story.html`) driven by a rig function of time: the student, camera and the morphing device
  (notebook page → laptop screen → tablet) are all keyed states, so nothing pops or cross-fades between "scenes".
- The cartoon uses the app's own ink (#2B2B2B), blue (#3B5BDB), yellow (#FFE45C) and handwriting face (Patrick Hand), so the
  intro hands off to the real product without a change of world.
- Bridge: the tablet's live waveform carries across into the dark question card, stretches into the full-width sound wave,
  then collapses into a yellow disc exactly where the real app's mic button is (960, 948); the real screenshot irises open from it.
