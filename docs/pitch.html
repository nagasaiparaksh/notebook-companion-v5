1The problem

  • Students write while the teacher talks, so they listen less and copy more.
  • Indian classrooms are spoken and code-mixed (Hinglish and more), but note apps are English-first and typing-first.
  • Generic speech tools mishear course terms ("are and ends" for RNNs).
  • Notes stay passive: nothing quizzes you or shows what you missed.

2The solution

A voice-first notebook: tap the mic once and talk. For every sentence the AI decides whether it is dictation, a command or a question, then writes, acts or answers.

Students upload their syllabus or slides; from then on recognition, answers, quizzes and "what did I miss?" all follow their course.

3Everything by voice

"Plants make glucose from sunlight…" dictation  ·  "Write a summary with a heading and a diagram"  ·  "Change the heading to…" "Undo that"  ·  "Explain my notes simply" spoken answer from your notes  ·  "Add that to my notes"  ·  "Quiz me" "Make flashcards" "What did I miss?"  ·  "Record the lecture" live captions → organised notes  ·  "Photosynthesis में plants…" Hindi + English in one sentence

4Gnani APIs used

Prisma v3.0 STT (REST)Dictation and commands; keyterms, topic, previous_text from the student's course material; code-mixed output
Prisma v3.0 Streaming STTLive lecture captions over WebSocket, relayed by the server (key never in the browser), with 15-min session rollover
Prisma v3.0 Batch STTUploaded lecture recordings with speaker diarization (teacher vs students)
Timbre v3.0 TTS (REST + SSE)Answers and read-aloud, streaming so speech starts in ~1 s; 13 voices, all supported languages

5What makes it different

  • Syllabus-aware recognition: course terms boost Gnani Prisma; a built-in A/B test shows the difference.
  • Code-mixed first: English + 9 Indian languages, mixes of them, or auto-detect.
  • Acts, not just transcribes: writes, edits, draws, quizzes, all undoable.
  • From passive to active recall: every notebook becomes a quiz, flashcards and a gap check.
  • Private: per-user accounts; export to PDF, Word or Markdown.
